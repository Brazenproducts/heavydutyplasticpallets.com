ISPM-15 and Why PP Export Pallets Avoid It

ISPM-15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) requires that wood packaging material crossing international borders be treated to eliminate wood-boring insect pests. The two approved treatments are heat treatment (HT, 56°C for 30 min core temperature) and methyl bromide fumigation (MB, being phased out). Both add cost, time, and supply chain complexity.

Who enforces ISPM-15: The EU, Australia, Japan, China, South Korea, New Zealand, Brazil, Canada, and over 180 other countries. The US enforces it for imports from those countries. PP pallets are exempt in all ISPM-15 enforcement jurisdictions — plastic is not regulated under ISPM-15.

Specification Guide for Heavy Duty Plastic Pallets

Choosing the right heavy duty plastic pallets requires matching wall thickness, flute profile, and resin grade to your specific application. Key specifications:

  • Wall thickness: 4mm for light duty, 6mm for standard, 8mm for heavy duty, 10mm for bulk container applications
  • Flute type: B-flute for top-load stacking strength, C-flute for balanced performance, X-flute (double wall) for maximum rigidity
  • Resin grade: Standard PP for ambient environments, high-density PP for cold chain and freezer applications down to -40°F
  • Food-grade: FDA 21 CFR-compliant virgin PP for direct food contact — required for produce, poultry, seafood, and processed food applications
  • Custom dimensions: Elipacko cuts to custom sizes with lead times varying by volume — contact directly for specifications
